# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.
Participation in this project means treating other people with care and
technical disagreements with curiosity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing observable impact.
- Welcome questions and make room for people with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, consent, and responsible security disclosure.
- Focus critique on ideas, evidence, and code—not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data or credentials, and retaliation
against good-faith reporters are not acceptable. Publishing exploit details in
violation of the security policy is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an
available repository contact channel. For a security vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the
safety of affected people. Enforcement decisions may consider severity,
repetition, intent, impact, and willingness to repair harm.
